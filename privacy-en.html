Privacy

Operator: Ivan Kukin (individual, Thailand) · growlog.app.support@gmail.com

On device data and exports

GrowLog stores garden and plant records, tasks, care, problems, measurements, harvests, supplies, photos and saved AI answers on your device. Location is optional. The current app has no GrowLog account or automatic garden sync through a GrowLog server. Device backups depend on your iOS settings.

User-created backups may include photos, saved AI answers and exact saved coordinates. GrowLog does not automatically upload them to its server. Copies exported through the system share sheet are handled by the destination you select.

Location and climate

Weather and climate features transmit exact garden coordinates for the selected location. GrowLog does not track movement in the background. Forecasts use Open-Meteo.

Climate requests go to GrowLog on Cloudflare. New profiles are prepared through GitHub Actions using Copernicus ERA5-Land and elevation data. Coordinates are used in build inputs and may appear in operational files; plant records are not needed. Profiles and coordinates are stored on Cloudflare for reuse. A prepared location profile may be accessible without signing in by providing its coordinates. It contains no garden journal, photos or owner name.

Prepared profiles are a shared climate dataset for a location and currently have no automatic deletion deadline. The association between a request and an access identifier is stored separately in usage accounting. Removing user-linked records does not automatically mean removing the shared profile for everyone. GitHub result artifacts are configured for 14 days; this does not establish the retention of all GitHub logs or provider backups.

AI requests and recovery

AI runs when you request it. A versioned local confirmation explains sharing before the first request and after an updated consent version. Cancelling sends no request. In Profile → About GrowLog → AI data sharing, you can reopen the disclosure and withdraw consent. After withdrawal, the next AI analysis requires confirmation again. Withdrawal does not erase data already sent or saved answers; garden records remain available.

Analyses may send crop, variety, stage, growing conditions, problem descriptions, observations, relevant care records and supply details through GrowLog to Cloudflare Workers AI. Free-text notes may contain personal information you entered.

AI Diagnosis and Growing Care do not receive the exact saved garden coordinates from the location field. They receive an approximate regional value rounded to 0.1 degrees. Current AI Diagnosis and Growing Care features do not upload photos. Coordinate rounding does not remove addresses or personal details from free-text notes.

When result recovery is used for a request, GrowLog temporarily stores the completed AI answer on Cloudflare so reconnecting does not create another analysis. This mechanism does not persist the original AI request body. The recovery period is 15 minutes from the time the answer is stored: after that period, the answer is no longer served, and automatic timer-based cleanup is configured to remove the server copy. This period applies only to the recovery copy of the answer, not to usage accounting, climate profiles, provider logs or backups. Answers saved in the app remain on your device until you delete them.

Cloudflare states that Workers AI content is not used to train models or improve services without explicit customer consent. Separate Cloudflare storage follows its configuration and provider terms. Workers AI data usage.

History for an external AI

The export is created locally without calling GrowLog AI. It includes dated facts about one plant, its conditions and readings for its current zone. Current garden coordinates are rounded to 0.1 degrees. Photos and GrowLog AI conclusions are excluded, but your notes remain. Review the text before copying or sharing. Another AI service processes submitted data under its own rules.

Allowances and purchases

GrowLog stores an access identifier, usage counters, operation identifiers and outcomes to enforce allowances and prevent duplicate debits. Climate accounting associates that identifier with a location key containing coordinates. The absence of an owner name does not make these records fully anonymous. This is not a complete garden backup.

When Apple purchases are used, purchase information and status are needed to verify Pro access. GrowLog does not request full payment card details. Cancelling a subscription, disabling device access and erasing data are separate actions.

When protected access through an Apple purchase is used, signed app and purchase information, product and transaction identifiers, subscription expiry and status are verified. Device registration through Apple App Attest uses a key identifier, public key, app authenticity evidence and technical counters. Registration records are linked to the subscription and allowances; lack of a name does not make them anonymous. Registration and disabled-access records have no automatic deletion deadline and are used to prevent reuse of disabled access. Disabling registration does not cancel the subscription or erase those records. This section applies when the corresponding access method is used.

Retention of service records

Old allowance records are pruned on later activity. Inactive access has no guaranteed deletion time. Shared climate profiles have no automatic deletion deadline. Records needed to prevent abuse and duplicate charges are considered separately when handling deletion requests. Provider logs and backups may have retention periods different from the AI response recovery period.

Notifications and deletion

With your permission, iOS schedules reminders locally. Garden names and task text may appear on the lock screen; control previews in iPhone notification settings.

Remove Location and Delete All Data are local controls. Delete All Data also resets local AI consent. These controls do not cancel an Apple subscription or automatically erase server climate profiles, usage accounting, provider logs or previously exported files. Contact the operator listed above about server-held data and applicable access, correction or deletion rights. Describe the feature and data in your request; do not send access keys, passwords or a full garden backup. The operator checks the requester's relationship to the records and applicable retention obligations. Disabling access and erasing data are separate actions.

The current app has no advertising, cross-app tracking or product analytics SDK. Providers may process connection data such as IP addresses; network requests are not represented as fully anonymous.

Your choices and rights

Sharing with AI requires separate confirmation in the app. You can decline or withdraw consent for subsequent requests. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing. Without data needed for a request, the corresponding online feature cannot fulfil it; the local journal remains available.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to information and a copy of personal data, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, data portability and a complaint to a competent data protection authority. Use the contact above to make a request. Responses and permitted extensions follow applicable statutory deadlines. Proportionate verification of your connection to the records may be necessary to protect your data; your Apple Account password and secret keys are not requested for this purpose. If a request cannot be fulfilled in full, you will be told the reason, subject to applicable legal restrictions.
