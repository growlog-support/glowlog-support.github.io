Support and feedback

Operator: Ivan Kukin (individual, Thailand) · growlog.app.support@gmail.com

Contact support

For questions about GrowLog, bug reports and suggestions for improving the app, email growlog.app.support@gmail.com.

Describe what you wanted to do and what happened. If possible, include your GrowLog version, iOS version and iPhone model. You can attach a screenshot with personal information hidden. Do not send passwords, verification codes, access keys or a full garden backup.

An analysis or calendar will not load

Check your connection and read the message in the app. If a limit reset time is shown, try after that time. Preparing climate data for a new location can take several minutes. Saved AI answers and prepared locations do not require a new analysis or build.

If the error persists, send its exact text and the approximate time it occurred, including your time zone. Your exact garden address is not needed for the initial request.

Using GrowLog offline

Records and AI answers already saved on your device remain available offline. New AI analyses, forecast downloads, retrieving climate profiles from the server and purchase verification require internet access. No new allowance charge does not mean server data is available offline. An incomplete or stale forecast does not confirm a suitable planting window.

Restoring purchases and a lost device

For an existing subscription, use Restore purchases in GrowLog Pro with the same Apple Account. This restores access, not your garden records. If it fails, send support the message and time; never send your Apple Account password or verification codes. If a phone is lost, contact support about disabling its server access. This does not remotely erase its garden or cancel a subscription.

How Pro allowances work

Problem analysis and care plans share an allowance of up to 5 analyses per day and 30 per month. The calendar includes up to 3 new locations per month. Allowances reset by UTC calendar day and month. Pro terms explain when units count and when they are returned.

Exporting plant history for your own AI does not use your GrowLog analysis allowance. Viewing saved results does not use it either.

Where your records are stored

Your garden, photos and saved results are stored on your device. The current version does not automatically sync your garden through a GrowLog account. Before changing phones or deleting the app, save a backup using the data export section in Profile. Restoring a subscription does not restore your garden journal.

Purchases and subscriptions

Subscription availability, price and billing period are shown in GrowLog Pro. The app will indicate if purchasing is not yet available.

You can manage an active Apple subscription in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the app or its data does not cancel a subscription. Apple instructions.

Questions about your data

For access, correction or deletion requests concerning data associated with you, use the same email address. Describe the feature and data involved. A description is enough for the initial request; do not send identity documents or secret access keys.

Deleting records in the app affects local data. Shared climate profiles and service records linked to an access identifier are considered separately. The Privacy Policy explains these distinctions.
