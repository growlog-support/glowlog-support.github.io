Pro features

GrowLog Pro includes four features:

  • AI problem analysis: possible causes and next steps based on the problem description and plant records. Current analyses do not send photos.
  • AI care plan: guidance based on the crop, stage, conditions and saved records.
  • Planting calendar: sowing and transplanting dates for a location where the data supports calculation.
  • Plant history for your AI: dated factual history to copy or share as a .txt file. This does not call GrowLog AI or use your analysis allowance. Another AI service has its own charges and terms.

Gardens, plants, journals, ordinary tasks, reminders and backups remain free. Your records and saved results remain accessible after Pro ends. This is not automatic cloud backup; keep backups of important records.

Allowances

Problem and care analyses share 5 analyses per calendar day and 30 per calendar month. The calendar allows preparation for 3 new locations per calendar month. Allowances reset in UTC, not local time or on the billing date. The allowance screen shows the next reset time. Unused allowances do not roll over.

Viewing saved results or retrieving an already prepared climate profile uses no allowance. Updating an analysis with new information is a new request. An automatic retry of the same AI operation does not debit twice; manually starting a new analysis is a separate request. A confirmed technical AI failure returns its allowance. An allowance refund is not a refund of the subscription payment.

A new location reserves one unit before preparation. Rejected admission releases it; polling the same location does not debit another unit. A confirmed admission remains counted even if a later build fails or the source cannot provide planting dates. Different exact coordinates may count as different locations within the same city.

Price and subscription management

The current price, currency and billing period are shown in the app before purchase. The annual subscription is billed in full for the year. Monthly and annual plans include the same features and allowances.

The plan name, period and full price are displayed before you confirm payment in the App Store. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. Manage them in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → GrowLog. Deleting the app or its data does not cancel a subscription. Apple instructions.

Turning off renewal preserves the paid period. New paid actions require an active subscription after it expires. Restore purchases through Apple using the same Apple Account; this does not recover your local garden without a backup. Restoring the same subscription or changing devices does not create a new monthly allowance.

Payment refund requests are handled through Apple under applicable rules and law. These terms do not limit mandatory consumer rights. If a special offer is available for your selected plan, its terms are displayed before purchase.

Guidance and availability

AI can be wrong and does not provide a confirmed diagnosis. Check the plant and product instructions. Climate dates are guidance, not a guarantee of weather, harvest or growing success. Some locations may lack sufficient data. New requests require internet and external services; saved local records remain accessible during service interruptions.

The Privacy Policy explains data sent for AI and climate features. Avoid unnecessary personal information in notes and review history before sharing it with another AI.
